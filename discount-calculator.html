How Discounts Work and Why They Boost Sales

Offering targeted promotional discounts, seasonal sales, and coupon codes is one of the most reliable strategies for e-commerce stores and brick-and-mortar retailers to acquire new customers, clear seasonal inventory, and raise average order values (AOV).

However, failing to calculate the compound impact of multiple stacked discounts can severely erode profit margins. Understanding discount mathematics ensures promotional offers remain lucrative.

Single vs Stacked Discount Formulas

Single Discount ($) = Original Price × (Discount % / 100)
Sale Price ($) = Original Price - Single Discount

Stacked Discount Sale Price ($) = Original Price × (1 - Discount1 %) × (1 - Discount2 %)
Effective Total Discount (%) = [ (Original Price - Final Sale Price) / Original Price ] × 100

Step-by-Step Worked Example: Black Friday Stacked Coupon

Example: Promotional Apparel Purchase

A shopper purchases a winter parka with an original sticker price of $200.00 during a Black Friday sale:

  • Primary Storewide Sale: 30% Off
  • VIP Promo Coupon: Additional 15% Off
  • Local Sales Tax: 7%
Price after 30% Storewide Sale = $200.00 × (1 - 0.30) = $140.00
Price after 15% VIP Coupon = $140.00 × (1 - 0.15) = $119.00
Total Dollars Saved = $200.00 - $119.00 = $81.00 (40.5% effective savings)
Final Checkout Total with 7% Tax = $119.00 × 1.07 = $127.33

Smart Discounting Rules for Retailers

  1. Set Minimum Cart Spending Thresholds: Tie high percentage discounts (e.g. 20% off) to minimum order values (e.g., "Spend $100 or more") to preserve net order margin.
  2. Beware of Coupon Stacking Glitches: Ensure your e-commerce checkout software prevents unintended combinations of clearance items with global percentage coupons.
  3. Use Dollar Off vs Percentage Off Wisely: For items priced under $100, percentage discounts (e.g., "25% off") sound more appealing; for items over $100, flat dollar savings (e.g., "$50 off") drive higher conversions.
