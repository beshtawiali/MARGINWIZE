About MarginWize

MarginWize was founded with a singular, focused objective: to provide clean, fast, accurate, and completely free financial calculation tools for small business owners, managers, accountants, and e-commerce sellers.

Our Core Principles

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your financial inputs, prices, costs, and tax amounts are processed entirely inside your browser. We never store or transmit your sensitive numbers.

Speed & Zero Bloat

Built with vanilla JavaScript and clean modern styling for instant calculations without heavy framework overhead or server delays.

Educational Clarity

Every tool includes formula breakdowns, worked math examples, and practical business advice to empower financial literacy.

Editorial & Accuracy Standards

Our financial formulas and articles are created and reviewed by finance and accounting professionals to align with standard GAAP and IFRS mathematical definitions.
